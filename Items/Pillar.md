The Pilar is a magical item very powerful. It is the very item binding [[Ether|The Ether]] from both [[Aethernam]] and [[Zephyrion]], at least.
It holds a contained black hole whose inside is [[Ether|The Ether]], and every Pillar opens onto that same Ether. This item absorbs all the magic generated by the living being, and send it to [[Ether|The Ether]]. It is also through this item that the [[Ether mirror]]s share their mana to the living being.

### Mechanics & Inner Workings

Even if the inner mechanism have never been uncovered, as the powerful magical flux flowing through it are very highly protected and disrupts the devices used to analyze it, the experiment 4551 allowed the recovery of a magical spark of unknown proprieties. Somehow, after the experiment 4551, the Pillar seemed to have adapted itself, and is has yet never been reproduced. All experiments on the pillar after the [[Experiment 6012]].
Even if no one in [[Aethernam]] nor [[Zephyrion]] knows it, it is a [[Shinit]] creation: the "dimensional catalyser" of their anti-magic project, built to drain the magic overflow that was killing them. See [[Magic]].

### Manufacturing process

The manifacturing process in only known by a few of the remaining [[Shinit]]

## Rarity

There is usually only one Pillar per star system. This is the only one in both [[Aethernam]] and [[Zephyrion]]. It used to stand on [[Aethernam]], and was moved to [[Zephyrion]] after [[The Great Cataclysm]], since leaving it in the long run on a scorched planet mostly ruled by monsters would have been too dangerous.

## Raw materials & Components

It is mostly made from an unknow material which seems to be really hard to cut, and somehow impossible to melt.
Only the [[Experiment 6012]] seems to have managed to damage it very slightly.
