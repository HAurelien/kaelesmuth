### Transmission & Vectors

This affliction is not easily given. Actually, there are only two ways to acquire it : through the [[God of Eldritch creatures]] (often on one's death), and having a lot of mana imbued in a very young child by a very skilled Vampire. The chances of success is very low, and it only works if the parents are already vampires, since it gives the child the ability to withstand so much mana.

### Causes

This affliction comes from the [[God of Eldritch creatures]] and the [[God of Progress]] which both tried to break the yet unknown magical concealment imposed by the [[Pillar]].

### Symptoms

The body slows down extremely quickly. The heartbeat is almost impossible to detect, hence the wounds won't bleed much, but they won't heal much easily.
The body age significantly slower, too: about 10x, making a vampire's life span of maximum about 3000 years. They also from rarely to never have any kind of sickness due to their biological part, but are very weak from magic affections.
However, that also means normal vampires born with this condition only reach adulthood at around 90yo (because they do age a bit faster when young, since they have very little magic powers because of their small underdeveloped brain).
Hence, they tend to be extremely smart when born as vampires, because their brain had a huge time to develop perfectly, they are very 'easy' to manipulate when young, but very hard to change later on.

### Treatment

There is no known treatment. Those who got the magic taken away from them or overused it simply died.

### Prognosis

Analyzing one's magic closely, or monitoring one's body reactions, can help detecting them.

### Affected Groups

Inhabitant of [[Aethernam]] before the Silence, when the gods arrived to [[Zephyrion]]. After that, they were present on both planets.

### Prevention

There is no way to prevent it as far as we know. However, a powerful entity such as an extremely skilled mage of a God could "protect" a cadaver from turning into it, given enough energy and research.

### History

They appeared around 1708, children of the [[God of Eldritch creatures]], in [[Aethernam]]. It took a few centuries of trials from him to stabilise a creature that is both sentient and survives long enough to develop.
Vampires born as such stay very rare. [[Angres Rimas]], whose parents are vampires, is one of these few newborn vampires.

### Cultural Reception

As the persons afflicted bear the feeling of death, they have always been rejected by society. Considered as a curse, vampirism is tolerated in most society by the law starting from 2000, but not to most of the religious group or citizens. As such, no country consider turning someone to a vampire against his own will as a legal act.
Before the 2000s, vampires where hunted more or less everywhere. The shift has been from the discovery of a way to harness mana through a tool, allowing for an easy exploitation of other creatures than humans because it drastically decreases the cost of the harnessing spell.
