# Magic

How magic works in Kaelesmuth, as physics. Notes about magical things ([[Ether]], [[Pillar]], [[Ether mirror]], [[Aura]], [[Elementary]], the gods) follow these rules.

## Mana comes from minds

Every living brain produces mana. The amount follows the total number of neural connections in use, not intelligence as such: a [[Human]] produces a lot, an elephant too (its brain is proportionally smaller but larger in absolute terms), an insect almost nothing. Intense thinking and strong emotion raise the output for a while.

Faith does not create mana. It gives it a direction: praying to someone sends one's mana to that person's [[Ether mirror]]. Everyone produces mana; believers decide where it goes.

## The Pillar and the Ether

A [[Pillar]] holds a contained black hole. Its interior is a sub-dimension, the [[Ether]]: a small universe made almost only of mana, with at best a few particles of ordinary matter that slipped through the containment.

- Mana falls into the Pillar from all around it, the way matter falls toward a mass. A Pillar drains mana over planetary distances.
- Every Pillar opens onto the same interior. That is why [[Aethernam]] and [[Zephyrion]] share one Ether, and why the Pillars form a network of passages between star systems.
- Mana has no mass (no Higgs coupling), so this "black hole of magic" has no real gravitational pull on matter and causes no time dilation. Time runs the same in the Ether as outside.
- The Pillars are [[Shinit]] creations. Their anti-magic project built the "dimensional catalyser" to drain the magic overflow that was killing them, so a Pillar is a seal by design: it leaves living beings almost no mana to cast with.

Mana goes back out of the Ether through the Pillar along one path only: an [[Ether mirror]] can send mana to the living being it is connected to, or to those who pray to it.

## Ether mirrors and gods

Each mind with enough connections has an [[Ether mirror]] in the Ether, fed by that mind's mana. The ambient mana of the Ether is corrosive: a mirror that is no longer fed dissolves into it within months of its living being's death.

A mirror that keeps being prayed to after death survives on its believers' mana, and can feed on other mirrors. That is a god. Gods are powerful because they pool the mana of many minds, and they depend on the living: if every living being died, the gods would starve and dissolve into the Ether.

The [[Higher gods]] are not mirrors. They come from far more advanced civilisations that broke out of the Ether into ordinary reality, and draw mana directly from the Ether through a black hole with their own technology. That makes them immensely powerful, and almost entirely uninterested in Aethernam, [[Arrack]] or the lower gods: to them, all of it is a grain of salt next to the Sun.

## Casting and its cost

- **Energy is conserved.** A spell never releases more energy than the mana spent on it. One brain only produces a trickle, so a lone caster's magic is small.
- **Overdrawing burns the caster.** Pushing past one's stored mana draws on the nervous system itself. Those who overuse magic, or have it taken from them, can die of it (see [[Vampirism]]).
- **Large effects need pooled mana:** a god's gift, worship, or tools that harvest mana from other creatures.
- **Every cast leaves a trace** of the caster's [[Aura]] on its target.

## Overflow

Mana that is not drained builds up in the environment. In high concentration it is dangerous to living tissue (the [[Shinit]] digestive system was especially vulnerable), and it organises itself into entities made of mana, the [[Elementary|Elementaries]], or twists living beings into creatures. This is what happened after [[The Great Cataclysm]] breached the Pillar's containment.

## History of magic on Aethernam

Under the Pillar, people kept too little mana to cast on their own. Before [[The incident]] (2753), magic only reached them as gifts sent by gods through their mirrors: [[God of Humankind|Absalom the First]], the sorceress who became the [[Goddess of Hope]], the first vampires. [[The incident]] is the first time a human cast magic with no god behind it, which is why it counts as the discovery of magic.

## Arrack

[[Arrack]] came from another star system, from a much more advanced civilisation, travelling through the Pillars and the Ether. She almost dissolved in its corrosive mana on the way. In the Ether she absorbed several gods, and with their knowledge and power she cursed [[Aethernam]] to force the gods to let her back into the Ether, so she could finish eating them.
