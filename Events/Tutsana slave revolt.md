## Summary

The slaves of Western [[Tutsana]] revolted during the Century of Silence, helped by the gods.

## What happened

During this attempt to take power, the administration of [[Eastern Tutsana]] massacred the city of [[Miari]].

## Consequences

Few survived, which is why Tutsana's population is still small. Western Tutsana became a free country governed by an elected assembly.
