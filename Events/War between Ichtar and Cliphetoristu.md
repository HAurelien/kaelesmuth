## Summary

An ongoing war between [[Ichtar]] and [[Cliphetoristu]], which Ichtar is winning in 1243. The front often moves back and forth through the east of Cliphetoristu.

## What happened

Ichtar sends armed groups to raid the east of Cliphetoristu; villagers who don't flee are usually massacred. [[Kyrith]] was besieged in 1235 and 1237 without falling.
