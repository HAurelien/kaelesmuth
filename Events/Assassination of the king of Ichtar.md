## Summary

The king of [[Ichtar]] was assassinated, a few months before the 1243 picture.

## What happened



## Consequences

It badly weakened Ichtar and disrupted its chain of command: Queen Sylsta rules while waiting for the heir to come of age, and the late king's cousin tries to seize the throne. Neither Cliphetoristu nor Sylsta took advantage of it. Many believe it is linked to [[Thara Morseth]] being chosen by [[Valzarr]].
