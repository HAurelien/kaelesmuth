These are the gods ruling the Universe. They are not [[Ether mirror]]s but entities from far more advanced civilisations, who broke out of the [[Ether]] into ordinary reality and draw mana directly from it through a black hole with their own technology (see [[Magic]]). They are immensely powerful and almost entirely uninterested in [[Aethernam]] or [[Arrack]]: to them, all of it is a grain of salt next to the Sun. They are able to create whole Galaxy out of the palm of their hands, and are the only one knowing how to create the devices splitting worlds and allowing you to leave the [[Ether|The Ether]].
