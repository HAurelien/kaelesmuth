## Summary

A country whose political landscape stays fairly obscure to foreigners.

## Geography

The west of the [[Western continent]]. Much of the country is desert, so its food depends heavily on fishing along the west coast and farming in the east. It is divided into three regions:

- **Western region**: lives from fishing, and stays fairly isolated from the war behind the vast desert, a natural barrier. When it can, it ships food and resources to the east.
- **Eastern region**: lives under constant threat of war. Its people are prepared and trained for combat, and many small villages are fortified and barricaded. The cities, especially those built around oases in the centre of the region, are often empty, kept as retreat points when battles are lost in the open field. Its people know the land and how to survive in the desert, so they outsmart and outlast attackers who venture there, and some of these fortresses are said to be virtually impossible to besiege. Chief city: [[Kyrith]].
- **Southern region**: protected from the conflict by the water and the mountains, which let the few attacks be contained at the border. It is the economic leader, with boats secretly trading with a few islands of [[Havland]], [[Thoear]] and the [[Stalaras Confederation]], which lets it supply the army of the north-east with food and weapons when needed.

## History

### In 1243

Its political landscape remains unclear to foreigners. Each region is governed by a respected leader chosen by the population rather than by formal election:

- Western region: [[Arik Selvar]], since 1240.
- Eastern region: [[Thara Morseth]], since 1243.
- Southern region: [[Liora Tanith]], since 1237.

Its people worship only [[Valzarr]].

It is at war with [[Ichtar]], which is winning (see [[War between Ichtar and Cliphetoristu]]). Armed groups from Ichtar often raid the east, threatening its farms. The loyalty of the people is a force to reckon with; villagers who fail to flee are usually massacred by Ichtar's army, hence the retreat points, as the front often moves back and forth.

Cliphetoristu had been regaining strength after the [[Assassination of the king of Ichtar]] a few months ago, but it did not seize that chance to strike a powerful blow, which is seen as a sign of weakness. Many believe that Thara Morseth being chosen by Valzarr last year and the king's assassination were no coincidence.

Apart from the war, little is known about this land, which gives rise to many strange, exaggerated and occult stories.

### Around 1300 (time of Alexendre X)

Its alliance with [[Tostep]] is starting to crumble as the country recovers from its intensive, exhausting war economy, now that [[Thoear]] has collapsed. See [[Fall of Thoear]].
